Tracie Leonhardt, DO

Tracie Leonhardt, D.O., is a leader in Integrative Medicine and founder of Peaks of Health Metabolic Medical Center. A Southeastern University graduate, she spent 15 years as a Board-Certified ER physician before shifting focus to metabolic and hormone systems after reversing her own Type II Diabetes. She holds an advanced fellowship in Anti-Aging and Regenerative Medicine and specializes in diabetes, thyroid, and complex conditions like Lyme disease and autoimmune disorders.

Dr. Leonhardt is an accomplished teacher, speaker, and author, serving on medical boards and contributing to journals like Townsend Newsletter. She has appeared on TV programs, including Daytime and Tampa Bay Morning Blend, and hosted a radio show with guests like Dr. Oz and Suzanne Somers. Her mission is to combine traditional and functional medicine to deliver exceptional patient outcomes globally.

Sherri Morrison, RN, BSN

Sherri Morrison is the founder and co-owner of Success by Design. She is a Registered Nurse, B.S.N. from Temple University in Philadelphia and brings over 30 years of nursing, nutrition, and administrative experience. Sherri had constant frustrations with fad diets. Not only do they seldom work, but they can also be dangerous. After losing her father to his fourth heart attack when he was 42, Sherri began streamlining her 31 years of medical and nutrition expertise to develop a program that eschews the catchall diets and wild promises of many popular programs. Sherri believes that her father, and many others like him, could prevent such tragedies and illnesses by taking control of their lifestyles and eating and exercise habits.
